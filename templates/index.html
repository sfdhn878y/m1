Welcome

This is the exam management home page. Teachers and admins can keep courses, exams, and marks in one place. Use Login or Register from the top to continue.

About this site

The site is made for a small college setup. An admin creates courses, adds exams for those courses, and sets a rubric so marking stays clear. Students and staff sign in with their own account.

What you can do

  • Register a new account or log in with your email and password.
  • Admins open the dashboard to manage courses and exams.
  • Create a course, then edit or remove it later if the details change.
  • Create an exam for a course and attach a rubric for checking answers.
  • Log out from the top menu when you are done.

How to get started

New users should register first. After login, an admin can go to Manage Courses and add a course name. From Manage Exams, add an exam and open the rubric page if you want marking points saved with that exam.

Contact us

Need help with login, a missing course, or an exam that will not open? Write to admin@gmail.com. Please include your name, email, and a short note about the problem so we can reply faster.

Office hours: Monday to Friday, 9:00 am to 5:00 pm.

Feedback

We like to hear from you. Send feedback, ideas, or bugs to the same email. Tell us which page you were on and what you expected to happen.

Good feedback helps us fix login issues, course lists, and exam pages. Thank you for using the site.
